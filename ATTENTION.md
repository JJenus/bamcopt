# Public-site review items

The public pages use conservative, general information where business details were not supplied. Review these items before a public launch.

- **Legal review:** `/legal/privacy` and `/legal/terms` contain standard baseline wording, not jurisdiction-specific legal advice. Confirm the registered operator, address, eligibility, governing law, complaints/ombudsman channels, financial regulatory disclosures, retention periods, lawful bases, cross-border safeguards, and applicable consumer rights. No license, deposit insurance, or investment guarantee has been asserted.
- **Public configuration:** Set the `NUXT_PUBLIC_*` variables documented in `docs/public-site.md`. Defaults preserve the Bancopt name/phone and assume `support@bancopt.com` and `https://www.bancopt.com`. Verify that the mailbox is monitored and the phone is correct. Legal entity defaults to Bancopt; business address is intentionally blank. Update `NUXT_PUBLIC_LEGAL_UPDATED` when policies change.
- **Investment availability:** No investment execution/product exists in this app. `/investment` is an enquiry/information page only. Replace with approved offering details and disclosures only if a real service is introduced.
- **Product terms:** Confirm supported destinations/currencies, fees, processing times, limits, eligibility, and funding reconciliation procedures. Pages intentionally avoid fixed rates, completion-time promises, invented metrics, reviews, or corporate-banking claims.
- **Support:** Contact uses real `mailto:` and `tel:` links, not a pretend submission form. No delivery or response-time promise is made. A ticket/contact endpoint and newsletter service are not included.
- **Privacy providers:** Public and authentication layouts no longer automatically load chat widgets. Existing protected layouts/integrations are out of scope. Audit all hosting, email, upload, font, and any enabled chat providers; add any required consent mechanism before enabling non-essential tracking.
- **Receipt privacy:** Transaction references open existing public receipts. Treat references as confidential. Review the existing receipt API's exposed fields and abuse protection separately; this redesign does not alter financial APIs.
- **Authentication:** Existing account/session and backend verification behavior is preserved. Registration policy agreement is enforced by the public form but is not stored as a versioned consent record by the backend. Add server-side evidence/version tracking if required.
- **Scope:** `/app`, `/admin`, their layouts, and financial logic are excluded. The shared full-size logo follows the env-selected brand and image without changing styling or account workflows. Existing transactional email templates retain their legacy branding and `APP_URL` setting; align these separately if rebranding the entire service, not just the public site.
- **Environment example:** `.env.example` was restored at the user's request and includes the public-site variables plus the optional `NUXT_BUILD_DIR` override. Replace example values in a private environment file; do not commit real credentials.
